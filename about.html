About

I’m drawn to cybersecurity because I like understanding how networks behave and how to defend them. Network awareness, monitoring, and SOC-style investigation are the parts that stick — figuring out what’s normal, spotting what’s off, and documenting it clearly.

I’m studying for a B.S. in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance at Western Governors University and hold CompTIA A+. Outside class I build and publish tools people can actually run: ESP32 firmware and Android apps for passive wireless observation, touch UIs, and field-ready lab practice — with authorized-use framing and docs hiring managers can try, not just read about.

Looking for: entry-level cybersecurity analyst, SOC, network defense, or junior cyber roles — remote or Southeast Missouri / regional.
