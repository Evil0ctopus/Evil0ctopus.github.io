Birth Sky

Where was the sky when you arrived? Enter a birth date, optional time, and place. You’ll get an astronomical night-sky map for that location and moment, plus a symbolic natal chart wheel for fun — not a prediction, not destiny, not science.

Privacy: birth date/time stay in your browser — they are never form-POSTed to this site. Network calls go to OpenStreetMap Nominatim (geocoding) and to Wikimedia/Wikipedia (on-this-day history and optional place snippets). Only calendar month/day appear in URL paths, and place names in search queries. Timezone is resolved locally from coordinates. Nothing is stored or uploaded to this site.

Unknown? Leave blank — we assume local noon.
